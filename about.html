Problems faced while trying to grow crops on Mars

There are many problems that make it quite difficult to achieve agriculture on a hostile planet like Mars. We have identified these problems and brought about solutions. Constant water and sunlight required - Mars is a planet with a lot of dust storms and low sunlight, which can abate the growth of plants. Furthermore, Mars has limited water resources and it can be costly to import water from Earth. High radiation - Due to the lack of an ozone layer, radiation is very high on Mars which can impede the development of plants. Nutrients and Heat - The sand on Mars is not carbon-based organic soil, but is iron oxide, so it is infertile and doesn't contain the required NPK nutrients to grow plants. Furthermore, Mars' temperature is freezing and so the lack of heat can further impede plant growth.
