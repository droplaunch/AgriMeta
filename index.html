Innovation:-

Agrimaton Mark 2

Growing plants on a planet like Mars can be said impossible, but with some innovative technology, it can be made a reality. To make this dream tangible, we had created the Agrimaton. But we have brought a revised version 1 of the project thus making it Agrimaton Mark-II. It is a mobile automaton that facilitates the needs of plants on a planet like Mars. It can grow corms, tubers and other plants on Mars and planets with similar conditions
